# IN AUTHORITY — Privacy Notice (Release Candidate)

Last updated: August 15, 2026

IN AUTHORITY does not require an account in this release candidate.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally in your browser:

- Prayer history, including the dates and congressional member IDs you marked as prayed for.
- An optional ZIP code used for the "My Representatives" lookup.
- A cached copy of the current congressional roster so the app remains usable after a temporary network failure.

This information is not sent to an IN AUTHORITY server in this release candidate.

## Third-party services

When you use "My Representatives", the ZIP code is sent to the configured civic lookup service (RepsContact) to identify representatives. If that lookup is unavailable, the app links you to the official House.gov lookup.

The app also requests the public current-member dataset published by the unitedstates/congress-legislators project.

## Future analytics

No analytics or advertising SDK is included in this build. Add them only after a separate privacy review and update this notice.

This document is a product privacy draft, not legal advice. Have counsel review the final policy before launch.
